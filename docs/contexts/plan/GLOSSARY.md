# Plan

Looking ahead: laying out the journey and planning the workouts that lead to its destination. Shown in the product as **Splitsmith**.

The English words here are canonical in code and docs; the Icelandic UI translates them (stage → áfangi, rest stop → áning, destination → markið).

## Language

### The journey

**Journey**:
The athlete's training on the way to a destination: "the Journey to X". An athlete has at most one active journey.
_Avoid_: Season, plan, programme

**Destination**:
The written goal a journey leads to. It may have a date and a measurable aim (a goal race, "sub-40 10k") or be open ("a strong base").
_Avoid_: Goal race, target, markið (in English)

**Open journey**:
A journey whose destination has no date or measurable aim yet. It ends when closed or when a new journey starts, and can later be given both.
_Avoid_: Off-season, unstructured training

**Waypoint**:
A race or test along a journey's route that is not its destination.
_Avoid_: Tune-up, B-race, checkpoint

**Stage**:
A block of training within a journey with a single purpose, typically 4–6 weeks (base, build, peak, taper).
_Avoid_: Training block, phase, mesocycle, leg

**Training week**:
A Monday-to-Sunday week within a stage, optionally with a focus and a target volume.
_Avoid_: Microcycle

### Workouts

**Workout**:
A session planned for a specific day. A workout is always planned, never something already done. Workouts belong to the athlete and fall within a journey and stage only by their date. Anyone in the coaching relationship can change one, and every change is kept in its history.
_Avoid_: Session, training (for planned work)

**Workout step**:
One part of a running workout (warm-up, work, recovery, cool-down) with a length given as distance, time or open, and an optional step target.
_Avoid_: Step (unprefixed), segment, interval, phase

**Repeat block**:
A group of workout steps done a given number of times, e.g. 6 × (1 km work + 90 s jog).
_Avoid_: Repeat (unprefixed), step repeats, loop, set

**Step target**:
The intensity a workout step aims for: a pace range, heart-rate zone, power range or perceived effort.
_Avoid_: Target (unprefixed), goal, intensity

**Rest stop**:
A day deliberately planned for rest. A day with nothing planned and nothing done is not a rest stop.
_Avoid_: Rest day, off day, áning (in English)

**Sent to watch**:
A workout that has been put on the athlete's watch. It becomes _out of date on the watch_ if changed after sending.

### Library

**Workout library**:
A coach's or athlete's own collection of workout templates.

**Workout template**:
A reusable workout in a library. Planning from it creates a copy; later changes to the template do not affect workouts already planned.
_Avoid_: Preset, saved workout
